Roomiest, and the only one that still fits a 5.1 file. Costs ~15 px of card height, which pushes the strength slider down and makes this card taller than the two beside it.
Many channels. A 5.1 file splitting three ways needs a fallback whatever we pick: per-channel numbers reach 390 px and trim at the minimum width. Measured fallback that fits everywhere is Chose sparse on 1, 3, 5 and peaks on 2, 4, 6. at 222 px, with the figures in the tool tip.

What does not change. The tool tip already lists every channel with its own numbers, and keeps doing so in all of these. The agreeing line — the ordinary case — is untouched.
